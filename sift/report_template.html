How the score is built

Influence

TracIn over checkpoints

I(zi) = Σt ηt ⟨∇L(zi;θt), Pt∇L(Dval;θt)⟩

Did a step on this demo push the policy toward or away from good behaviour? Dval is the policy's own sim rollouts, relabelled by the expert. Pt is Adam's preconditioner.

Redundancy

Duplicates in action space

Effector path plus commands, resampled; blocked with Euclidean distance, confirmed with DTW, joined by union-find. The best-scoring member of each cluster is kept.

Consistency

Counterfactual instruction judge

A cross-fitted policy scores each demo's actions under every instruction. If another instruction explains the motion 3× better than its label, the label is flagged.

Ranking

Pessimistic order

Keep, then redundant, harmful, noisy, mismatch. Inside each tier, sort by mean − 1 sd influence across seeds, so a demo must help consistently to rank high.
